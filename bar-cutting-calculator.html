Bar and tube cutting calculator

Pick a stock length or type your own. Prices are optional. Up to 20 lengths and 400 cuts.

How to use it

  1. Bar length. The length your stock comes in, in millimetres.
  2. Kerf. Your saw’s cut width: about 1.5–2mm on a metal bandsaw, 2–3mm on a cold saw or mitre saw.
  3. Price per bar (optional) gives the material cost.
  4. Lengths. Each length you need and how many.
  5. Calculate. Each bar is drawn with its cuts. Offcuts of 500mm or more are shown in green as worth keeping.

This is the classic cutting stock problem. CutNest solves it and checks the answer against a lower bound, so when it says provably optimal, no plan uses fewer bars.
