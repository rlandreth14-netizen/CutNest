✒ One quick question

What cuts your material? This sets the kerf — the strip of material each cut destroys. Get it wrong and your sheet count will be wrong on every job. You can change it any time in Settings.

♻ Offcut stock

Usable pieces you have kept off previous jobs. When you start a job in the same material, these are offered as a remnant so you cut them before buying a new sheet.

📋 Paste your cut list
