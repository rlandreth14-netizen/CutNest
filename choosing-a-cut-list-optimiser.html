Buyer’s checklist

Choosing a cut list optimiser: 12 questions to ask

Most tools can pack rectangles onto a sheet. What separates them is whether the result fits how your shop buys, cuts and quotes. Ask these of any tool you try — including this one.

The checklist

1

Does it use the sheet sizes and prices you actually buy?

A tool that only knows one sheet size can’t tell you that two half sheets are cheaper than a full one.

CutNest: Up to 12 sizes per material with prices and “I have 6 of these” stock limits (2 sizes on the free plan); it picks the cheapest mix.

2

Does it tell you when a layout is as good as it gets?

Without a bound you never know if a better nest exists.

CutNest: Every result is checked against a mathematical lower bound and marked provably optimal when it hits it.

3

Can it cut for a saw, not just a CNC?

A nest a panel saw can’t cut is no use to a joiner.

CutNest: Guillotine mode with a numbered, edge-to-edge cut sequence on the cut sheet (Pro); free placement for CNC.

4

Does it respect grain, per part?

Veneered and brushed faces must not rotate; hidden parts should.

CutNest: Grain lock per material, and per part (Pro).

5

Does it handle bar, tube and extrusion?

Most jobs have some section in them.

CutNest: Yes, on every plan: stock lengths, end trim, saw kerf per section, and a saw list per pattern.

6

Can it get your cut list in without retyping?

Retyping 60 parts is where mistakes come from.

CutNest: Paste from email or a spreadsheet, or open CSV and Excel files; header rows are read by column.

7

Does it keep usable offcuts?

The cheapest sheet is the one already on the rack.

CutNest: Usable offcuts are measured and can be saved to stock and offered back on the next job (Pro).

8

What do you hand the workshop?

The office’s answer has to reach the machine intact.

CutNest: Dimensioned cut sheets, saw lists, labels for every part (Pro), CSV, PDF and DXF (Pro).

9

Can it price the job?

The sheet count is only half a quote.

CutNest: Pro turns the layout into a quote: material with markup, machine time from the actual cuts, handling, extras, VAT and your logo.

10

Where does your data live?

Cut lists and prices are commercial information.

CutNest: In your browser. No account; nothing is uploaded; the app works offline once loaded.

11

Millimetres or inches?

Your supplier and your tape measure should agree with the software.

CutNest: Both, with fractions (23 5/8, 8′ 6) and metres (2.4m).

12

What does it cost for the whole team?

Per-seat pricing adds up in a busy shop.

CutNest: Free for one material per job; Pro is £12 a month per company and the key can be shared with your team.
