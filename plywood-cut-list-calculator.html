How to use it

  1. Board size. 8×4 (2440×1220mm) is selected. Tap another size or type your own.
  2. Kerf. Your saw blade’s width, usually about 3–4.5mm on a panel saw and 2–3mm on a track saw. On a CNC router, use the cutter diameter.
  3. Price per board (optional) gives the material cost.
  4. Panels. Finished width, height and quantity. For veneered ply, oak-faced MDF or woodgrain melamine, untick “Parts can turn” so every panel keeps the grain running the same way.
  5. Calculate and you get the board count and every sheet drawn to scale.
