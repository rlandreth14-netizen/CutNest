FAQ

Straight answers

Will it work for saw cutting and guillotine, not just punch press?
Yes. Set the cutting method per material. “Free placement” for CNC punch, laser and router. “Guillotine” for saw or shear cutting where every cut goes edge to edge. Works for timber, steel sheet, acrylic — mix both in the same job.
Can I use my own sheet sizes and prices?
Yes — that’s the whole point. Go to Library, enter your sheet sizes and prices. Steel, MDF, ply, acrylic, glass — whatever you work with. CutNest uses your actual numbers. Pro users get a pre-built library of common UK grades as a starting point.
What if my material has a grain direction and can’t be rotated?
Tick “No rotation” on that material in your library. Works for brushed stainless, veneered MDF, wood grain, patterned glass — any material where rotation matters. CutNest will never flip pieces against the grain.
Do I need to install anything or create an account?
No to both. Open cutnest.co.uk/app in any browser and start using it. Your library and job history are saved in your browser — nothing is sent to a server. For Pro, you just enter a licence key in Settings after purchase.
How does the Pro licence work?
After payment you’ll get a licence key by email within a minute. Open the app, tap Settings, paste the key. Pro unlocks immediately. One key works for your whole company — share it with your team.
What happens if I cancel?
Monthly plans cancel at the end of the current billing period — no awkward process, just cancel from your Lemon Squeezy account. You keep access until the period ends. Annual plans are non-refundable but access continues until the year is up.
