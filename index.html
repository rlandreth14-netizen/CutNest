How it works

Three steps. No training needed.

If you can fill in a spreadsheet, you can use CutNest. There’s nothing to install and no account to create.

1
Add your pieces
Type them in, paste from an email, or open your CSV or Excel cut list. Millimetres or inches and fractions. Works for steel, timber, MDF, acrylic, glass — any sheet material.
2
Hit calculate
CutNest works out the most efficient way to fit everything onto the fewest sheets. Most jobs take a second or two, and it all runs in your browser.
3
Order with confidence
You get an exact sheet and bar count, total material cost and a visual layout showing where every piece goes. Print it for the shop floor, or turn it into a customer quote.

Grain direction lock
Brushed stainless, veneered MDF, patterned glass, wood grain — some materials can’t be rotated. Lock the grain on a whole material, or set it part by part: lock the doors, let the shelves turn. CutNest never flips a locked piece against the grain.

Cost-aware optimisation
Enter your sheet prices and CutNest minimises your total spend — not just sheet count. A cheaper smaller sheet that creates more waste isn’t always better. CutNest knows the difference.

Guillotine mode
Running a saw or guillotine as well as a punch press or laser? Set cutting method per material. Free placement for CNC work, guillotine mode for straight cuts. Ideal for timber, decking and steel sheet alike. Comes with a numbered cut sequence for the saw.

Free placement uses the MaxRects algorithm to pack pieces anywhere on the sheet — the same approach used in industrial nesting software. It’s ideal for CNC punch presses, plasma cutters, lasers, and routers where you can cut in any direction.
