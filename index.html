FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to install anything?
No. NestIQ runs entirely in your browser — Chrome, Edge, Safari, any modern browser. No downloads, no installation, no IT department needed.
What happens after I pay?
You'll receive a licence key by email immediately. Enter it in the Settings panel inside the app and Pro features unlock instantly. The key works per company — share it with your whole team.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel at end of the current billing period. Annual plans are non-refundable but you keep access until the year is up. No awkward cancellation hoops.
Is this for sheet metal specifically?
Yes. NestIQ uses free-placement packing — the correct algorithm for CNC turret punch press and laser cutting. Generic cutting optimisers often use guillotine algorithms designed for saws, which produce inferior results for punch/laser work.
What's the kerf setting?
NestIQ defaults to 20mm kerf (clearance between parts) which is typical for turret punch work. You can change this in Settings to match your machine and process.
Does it work offline?
Yes — once the page has loaded, the optimiser works with no internet connection. Shared library sync requires internet but all calculations run locally in your browser.
