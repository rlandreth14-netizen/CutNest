FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to install anything?
No. CutNest runs entirely in your browser. No downloads, no installation, no IT department needed. Works on Chrome, Edge, Safari and any modern browser.
What happens after I pay?
You'll receive a licence key by email immediately. Open the app, click Settings, paste your key — Pro unlocks instantly. One key works for your whole company.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel at end of the current billing period with no penalty. Annual plans are non-refundable but access continues until the year is up.
What's the difference between free placement and guillotine?
Free placement lets parts go anywhere on the sheet — correct for CNC punch press and laser cutting. Guillotine mode makes every cut go edge to edge across the full sheet, which is how a guillotine shear physically works. CutNest lets you set this per material so you can mix both in one job.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Once the page has loaded, all calculations run locally in your browser with no internet connection needed.
