2. The Service

CutNest is a browser-based cut list optimisation tool for sheet materials including steel, timber, acrylic and glass. It is provided as-is for professional and commercial use. A free tier and a paid Pro subscription are available. The free tier calculates one material per job using free placement. Pro adds multiple materials per job, grain lock, guillotine mode with cut sequences, remnants and offcut stock, the pre-built UK material library, DXF export, job history and the unit multiplier.
