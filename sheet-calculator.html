Sheet nesting calculator

Pick a standard size or type your own. Prices are optional. Up to 20 part sizes and 400 parts.

How to use it

  1. Sheet size. Tap a standard size or type the width and height in millimetres.
  2. Kerf. The gap you leave between parts: a few millimetres on a laser, more on plasma. The kerf guide has typical figures for each process.
  3. Price per sheet (optional) gives the material cost of the job.
  4. Parts. Width, height and quantity for each part. Untick “Parts can turn” for brushed or grained finishes.
  5. Calculate. You get the sheet count, the material cost, how much of each sheet is used, and every sheet drawn to scale.

The answer is checked against a mathematical lower bound. When it says provably optimal, no layout from any software uses fewer sheets. The yellow line shows what the usual “total area ÷ sheet area” sum would have said.
